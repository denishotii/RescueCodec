# Android app design

Surface: native Android, Jetpack Compose, Material 3. Mode: Operate (a tool used outdoors, in a hurry, with gloves). Familiar Material structure, brand in the theme.

## Use scene
A volunteer or rescuer in a forest or on a slope. Cold hands, gloves, bright sun or a dark night, one hand on the phone, often wet. Reports are short and urgent. Someone may be hurt. Nothing here may need two hands or fine aim.

## World
An emergency tool, not a fun app: easy to read at a glance, few elements, big text, plain words, no decoration. It reads like a normal messenger, because everyone already knows how to use one.
- Plain neutral surfaces: white by day (chat ground `#F1F3F5`, bubbles and bars `#FFFFFF`), near black by night (`#0B0C0E`, `#16181B`). High contrast for sunlight. The phone's setting decides; Settings can force Day or Night.
- Colour only for meaning, with the meanings everyone knows: **red** is Mayday, SOS, urgent and errors; **amber** is open, someone must act; **green** is done, OK and connected; **blue** is accepted and in progress, and the one accent (my own bubbles, primary buttons, the selected tab); **grey** is neutral info and times. Sender names get their own six hues (purple, teal, brown, pink, slate, olive) that never look like a state.
- Type: Barlow for text and labels, Barlow Condensed for titles. Bundled in `res/font`, no remote fonts. Body 18 sp, nothing below 13 sp.
- Shapes: bubbles 18 dp with a 6 dp tail corner, cards 16 dp, buttons 10 dp. One hairline on cards from others, no shadows, no gradients.
- Our performance story stays visible but quiet: under each of my messages a grey caption such as "11 B · 5.5× smaller than text", and while typing a green line says "105 bytes as text → 23 bytes sent".
- Every number in Airtime and savings says **measured** (solid outline, a check) or **estimated** (dashed outline). The word is always there.

## Rules we hold to
- Touch targets: 48 dp at least, 56 dp for the actions that matter (SOS, Send, mic, Accept, Done).
- One hand: the field, the mic and Send sit at the bottom, where the thumb is, with the SOS strip right below them.
- SOS is hold to confirm: 2 seconds, filled by the frame clock (so "Remove animations" cannot make it instant), haptic ticks, a screen reader gets it as a long press. A tap does nothing. It is always one hold away: a red strip above the tabs on every screen ("Hold 2 seconds for SOS"), slimmer on top of the keyboard while typing. While it is held, the strip fills with dark red and says "Keep holding", because the thumb hides part of it.
- A Mayday is never hidden: a red banner under the top bar shows on every screen until it is answered. On Tasks the Mayday card is on top instead, and while the keyboard is up the banner steps aside.
- Colour is never the only signal: words and icons come with every state.
- Language: English by default. Settings, Language switches the whole app and the decoded text to German. All strings are in `res/values` and `res/values-de`. A unit test checks both files have the same keys and placeholders.
- Offline: no CDN, no remote fonts, no network call anywhere in the UI.
- With the keyboard up, the tabs go away and the composer and a slim SOS strip ride on the keyboard.
- New things are loud only when they matter. A text from the team: a notification on "Messages" (default sound, short vibration) when the app is not in front; in front, a short tone, a haptic tick, and the chat follows it or shows a "New messages" pill. A new task: "Tasks" channel, high importance. A Mayday: its own loud "Mayday" channel. Accept or Done on my own report: a gentle "Answers" notification. Never for my own messages, duplicates or retransmissions. Settings has a Sounds switch for the in-app tone.

## Screens
Three tabs in the bottom navigation (a rail from 600 dp wide): **Chats** (badge: unread messages), **Tasks** (badge: open tasks and Maydays) and **Radio**. Settings opens from the gear in the top bar. Inside a chat the tabs go away.

- **Top bar**: one status pill with a coloured dot, the radio status ("Radio connected, D-Node 2", "Connecting", "No radio. 2 waiting.") and the screen name. Tap it for Radio. Then the gear for Settings. Inside a chat: a back arrow, the pill with the chat name, and a ⋮ menu with the chat's text mode: Auto, Always compressed, Always normal (not on the public channel, which is always normal).
- **Mayday banner**: red, under the top bar, until the Mayday is answered. Tap: Tasks. Accept or Done right in the banner.
- **Chats**: the chat list, like a messenger. Under "Channels on your radio": the team channel pinned on top, then the other private channels, then the public channel only when Settings "Show public channel" is on. Under "Direct": one row per person, then "+ Direct message". Each row: name, newest message, time, unread count. The app opens straight into the last chat; Back goes to the list.
- **A chat**: newest at the bottom. My messages on the right in blue with status ticks (clock waiting, one tick sent, two ticks relayed, ! not confirmed or failed; the state in words when it needs attention, tap for the explanation). Others on the left with the sender's name (team label from their answers, else "Node 1a2b"). Reports (found, request, hazard, sector, mayday) are compact cards: icon, plain title, field words, grid reference and distance, task state, Accept and Done (Decline smaller, apart). A Mayday card is red. Answers are small centred lines ("Trupp 3 accepted · Person found"). Day separators. Long-press: Read aloud, Copy, Details (size, bytes on air, signal, route, Cancel or Send again). No radio: an amber line at the top, "No radio connected. Messages wait. Connect". Direct messages and the public channel carry text only.
- **Composer**: one field ("Message or report"), the mic (hold to talk, contour rings while listening) when the field is empty, Send when there is text. While the field is empty, quick buttons (+ Person found, + Need, + Hazard, + Sector) sit above it. As soon as the text becomes a report, "What will be sent" shows the chips to change the type or a field (bottom sheets), "+ Add", "Send as plain text instead" and a green byte line ("105 bytes as text → 23 bytes sent"). For a text the line says how it goes, for example "Compressed for RescueCodec · 43 B instead of 81 B" ("We are at the hut, two people with us, waiting for the vehicle on the forest road", no GPS fix), "Compact words · Medical · 7 B (as text 19 B)" ("Apply a tourniquet." with compact text on) or "Normal Meshtastic text · readable by everyone".
- **Tasks**: Mayday first, then Open, Accepted by you, Taken by other teams, and Done folded away. Big Accept and Done.
- **Radio** (tab, or tap the status pill): Bluetooth scan and pair, reconnect, Wi-Fi radio or simulator (address and port), disconnect, the channel line and picker, channel QR code and pasted link, permission prompts and hints. New channel (a name of at most 11 bytes; a fresh 32-byte key in a free secondary slot; the QR opens right after). Tap a private channel: Show QR code (only that channel, Share link, the warning that the code is the key) and Remove channel (never the primary; a second confirm for the team channel; its messages stay hidden on the phone). At the bottom, Airtime and savings.
- **Settings** (the gear): Language, Display, Sounds, Team label, Radio and region (with "Show public channel", "Use compact encoding for text messages", and "Team vocabulary" while compact is on), Speech models, Other apps ("Let other apps send reports"), Battery settings, About.
