One shape: the tag

Every message is a tag with one cut corner. The cut points to the sender: left for others, right for you.

SOS is the only octagon

A stop-sign shape nothing else uses. Always bottom left, on every screen. Hold 2 s to send.

Colour means one thing

People get a colour mark by their name. Reports get a state band with the word in it.

My words are ink

My messages are ink, not blue. Blue only ever means accepted.

Kraft, not white

White tags stand out on kraft in the sun, and it feels like field gear. Night: warm charcoal.

Builds in 1.5 h

CutCornerShape for every shape, two bundled fonts, flat fills, no shadows. Icons: 2 dp stroke, square ends.
