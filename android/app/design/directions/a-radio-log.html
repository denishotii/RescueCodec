A log, not bubbles

Time in a fixed column, then the call-sign, then the words. Everything reads top to bottom. Your own lines carry a hi-vis YOU tag.

Colour is state

Red Mayday, amber open, blue accepted, green done. Always with a word and an icon. Hi-vis means "you": your tab, your key, your lines.

Two keys, like a radio

SOS sits bottom left on every screen (hold 2 s). Talk sits bottom right and turns into Send when you type. Black keys act, coloured blocks report.

Channels you tune into

Channels are dark keys with their number. Direct chats are outlined keys. No avatars, no photos, nothing to decode.

Radio is one tap

The status line under every title opens Radio. The menu (three dots) holds Radio, Airtime and Settings. Nothing else up there.

Fast to build

Fonts bundled in res/font. Flat fills, 2 dp outlines, 6 to 10 dp corners, no shadows. Night mode keeps the body and dims the display to graphite.
