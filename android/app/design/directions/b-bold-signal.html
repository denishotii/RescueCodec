Two bands, every screenHi-vis on top: where you are and the radio link (tap it for Radio; the menu holds Radio, Airtime, Settings). Ink at the bottom: Chats, Tasks, SOS.
The red cornerSOS is always the red square bottom left, also above the keyboard. Hold 2 s. Nothing else on screen is that red except a Mayday.
Chat as a logTimes in a left column, other people's words as plain text, yours as ink blocks. No avatars, no bubbles, no tails.
Reports by type, not boxesA wide bold title and one square in the state colour, always with its word: Open, Accepted, Done, Mayday.
The byte barWhile you write, a solid bar (23 B on air) sits over an outlined one (105 B as text). The logo is the same two bars.
Compose in 1.5 hRow, Column, Box, 3 dp corners, no shadows. Fonts in res/font: Archivo Expanded ExtraBold, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400 and 700.
