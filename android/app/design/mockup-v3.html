Colours only carry meaning: red Mayday/SOS, amber open, blue accepted and my messages, green done/connected. Byte savings only where you send (composer) and in details. Dark mode follows the same layout.

Chats. One row per private channel on the radio, then direct messages. Team channel pinned on top. Mayday shows as one slim red row, not a big banner. The app reopens the last chat directly.

Chat. Normal bubbles. Reports are bubbles with a title, two short lines and a distance, plus Accept/Decline buttons under them. Answers are small grey lines in the middle. A Mayday is pinned under the title bar with an Accept button.

Sending. One field. While you type, the app shows what it will send and the byte saving. Tap ✕ on a chip to remove it, ✕ on the strip to send as plain text. Mic when the field is empty.

SOS. Small red button in the title bar on every screen. Hold for 2 seconds. Radio, Airtime and Settings live in the ⋮ menu; the green dot opens the Radio screen.

Tasks. Compact rows with one button. Open, Mine, Done as filters. Red Mayday always first.

Removed from view. UTM grid numbers (tap the distance for the map and grid), "delivery unknown" labels (only a grey clock tick), byte captions under every bubble (only in the composer and in Details).
