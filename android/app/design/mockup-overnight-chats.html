What stays from overnight. Colours, fonts, shapes, the status pill with the screen name, the red Mayday banner with Accept, the red "Hold 2 seconds for SOS" strip, Grid chips, the green "bytes as text → bytes sent" line, the dark mic with its ring, night mode.

What changes. Report, History and Stats tabs are gone. Chats replaces Report and History: you read and write in the same place, one chat per radio channel plus direct messages. Tasks is the old Actions. Radio keeps scan, pairing, Wi-Fi, channel QR, and now also the airtime and savings numbers.

Writing. One field at the bottom of every chat. As soon as the text becomes a report, "What will be sent" appears with the chips and the byte line, exactly like the overnight Report screen. Mic when the field is empty, Send when there is text. Plain text in direct and Dräger chats.
